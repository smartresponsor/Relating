= Product Capability Audit: Relating
:revdate: 2026-09-20

== Boundary verdict

Relating owns CRM-oriented relationship lifecycle and relationship-derived business state, not party master data or generic CRUD. Vendor/customer/account identity stays with the corresponding owner component.

== Capability matrix

[cols="3,1,4",options="header"]
|===
|Capability |Status |Audit
|Relationship identity |PARITY |Explicit business relationship boundary exists.
|Relationship lifecycle/transitions |AHEAD |Dedicated lifecycle is richer than the relationship surface of typical commerce cores.
|Relationship timeline/history |AHEAD |Explicitly part of the business boundary.
|Scoring |AHEAD |CRM-oriented scoring is outside most commerce-core baselines.
|Conversion/progression |AHEAD |Explicit lifecycle capability.
|Relationship type/role |PARITY |Needed to express vendor/customer/partner/etc. links without owning their identities.
|Bidirectional links |PARTIAL |Directionality and reciprocal semantics should be explicit.
|Effective dates |PARTIAL |Useful for temporal relationships.
|Evidence/source/provenance |PARTIAL |Important when relationships are derived/imported.
|Merge/deduplication references |PARTIAL |Identity merge belongs to identity owner; relationship rewiring belongs here.
|===

== Boundary risks

* Vendor, customer, account and other party identities are external references.
* Generic CRUD remains Cruding.
* Avoid turning relationship scoring into generic analytics ownership.

== Milestone direction

M1: relationship type/direction/effective-date invariants.
M2: provenance, timeline and reciprocal-link semantics.
M3: identity-merge/relink acceptance with owning party components.
